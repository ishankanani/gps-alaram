## Full description (max 4000)

You know the feeling: eyes heavy after a long shift, the bus rocking, and the fear of waking up three stops too late. StopWake is a GPS alarm that wakes you just before your stop, so you can close your eyes, read, or simply stop watching every station go by.

WHY PEOPLE USE STOPWAKE
• After a night shift or a long day: doze off on the way home and still get off at the right stop.
• New city, foreign language: no need to catch every announcement or read every sign.
• Underground without signal: stops and the alarm work offline.
• Commuting with kids or a full head: one less thing to keep an eye on.
• Every day: give your attention back to a book, a podcast or the person next to you.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick your stop on the map or search for it: any station, stop or address.
• Choose when to wake: 1 to 15 minutes before you arrive, or 100 metres to 2 km before the stop.
• Lock your phone. StopWake follows your ride by GPS and rings in time, even on silent.

AN ALARM THAT ACTUALLY WAKES YOU
• A real alarm on your lock screen, with sound and vibration, even when the phone is on silent.
• Gentle, Normal or Heavy sleeper: Heavy sleeper reaches full volume within seconds.
• Snooze, or slide to dismiss.
• Keeps going in tunnels: when GPS drops, StopWake estimates your position and still wakes you.
• Easy on the battery: it checks less often while you are far away and more often as you get close.

1.4 MILLION STOPS IN 17 COUNTRIES, OFFLINE
Every bus, tram, metro and train stop in Germany is built in. One download each adds Austria, Switzerland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Belgium, France, the UK, Ireland, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, the USA, Canada, Australia and Japan. Zoom out on the map to see every stop StopWake knows. Search and the alarm work without internet, and you can save the map of your area for offline use too.

YOUR ROUTINE, ONE TAP AWAY
• Star the stops you ride every day and start the alarm in one tap.
• Recent stops and a history of every trip: how far you rode and when it rang.
• "Ring when I leave here": an alarm as soon as you move away from a place.
• Try a demo trip to see and hear the alarm before your first real ride.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your location is used only while a trip is running, and the alarm is decided on your phone.
• No account needed. An optional free account syncs your favourites and trips between phones.
• No background location: StopWake shows a notification whenever it uses GPS.

11 LANGUAGES
English, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Nederlands, Dansk, Svenska, Norsk, Suomi, हिन्दी and 日本語.

FREE, WITH ADS
StopWake is free and shows a few ads, never on the trip screen, the alarm or while it rings. Want a break? Watch one video in Settings for 24 hours without ads.

Good to know: GPS can be inaccurate underground, and some phones stop apps to save battery. StopWake's setup checks these settings for you. Please don't rely on StopWake as your only way to get off at the right stop.
