## Full description (max 4000)

Fell asleep on the bus again? StopWake wakes you just before your stop, so you can close your eyes, read or listen to music without watching every station go by.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick your stop on the map or search for it, any station, stop or address.
• Choose when to wake: 1 to 15 minutes before you arrive, or 100 metres to 2 km before the stop.
• Lock your phone and relax. StopWake follows your ride by GPS and rings in time, even in silent mode.

MADE TO WAKE YOU UP
• A real alarm on your lock screen, with sound and vibration, even when the phone is on silent.
• Gentle, Normal or Heavy sleeper: Heavy sleeper reaches full volume within seconds.
• Snooze, or slide to dismiss.
• Keeps working in tunnels: when GPS drops, StopWake estimates your position and still wakes you.
• Saves battery while you are far away and checks more often as you get close.

OFFLINE STOPS IN 17 COUNTRIES
Every bus, tram, metro and train stop of Germany is built in. Download the stops of Austria, Switzerland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Belgium, France, the UK, Ireland, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, the USA, Canada, Australia and Japan in Settings. Searching stops and the alarm work without internet, for example underground. Save the map of your area for offline use too.

YOUR STOPS, ONE TAP AWAY
• Star your favourite stops and start the alarm in one tap.
• Recent stops and a history of every trip: how far you rode and when it rang.
• "Ring when I leave here": an alarm when you move away from a place.
• Try a demo trip to see and hear the alarm before your first real ride.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your location is used only while a trip is running, and the alarm is decided on your phone.
• No account needed. An optional free account syncs your favourites and trips between phones.
• No background location: StopWake shows a notification whenever it uses GPS.

11 LANGUAGES
English, Deutsch, Français, Italiano, Nederlands, Dansk, Svenska, Norsk, Suomi, हिन्दी and 日本語.

FREE, WITH ADS
StopWake is free and shows a few ads. There are never ads on the trip screen, the alarm or while it rings. Want a break? Watch one video in Settings for 24 hours without ads.

Good to know: GPS can be inaccurate underground and some phones stop apps to save battery. StopWake's setup checks these settings for you. Please don't rely on StopWake as your only way to get off at the right stop.
