Delete your StopWake account

StopWake by Ishan Kanani · You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open StopWake and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete account and enter your password to confirm.
  3. Your account and its data are deleted immediately.

Without an account, Account → Delete my data removes everything StopWake stored on your phone.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address of your account, or include your StopWake ID (for example SW-7K3P-92QX, shown in the Account tab). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, password, display name, StopWake ID, language and country.
  • Synced data: favourites, recent stops and trip history.
  • Your devices and sign-in sessions, plans, gifts and redeemed codes.

What is kept

  • Nothing in our database. Backups that still contain your data expire within 30 days.
  • Data on your phone stays until you uninstall the app or use “Delete my data”.
  • Ad data held by Google is governed by Google’s policies; you can reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
