Delete your StopWake account

You can delete your account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open StopWake and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account (or Delete data if you never created an account).
  3. Confirm. Your data is deleted immediately.

By email

If you no longer have the app, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the email address of your account, or include your StopWake ID (for example SW-7K3P-92QX, shown under Account). We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your StopWake ID, email address, name and password.
  • Your plan history, gifted plans and redeemed codes.
  • Your devices and sign-in sessions.

Saved stops and trip history live only on your phone; uninstalling the app removes them. Backups that still contain your data expire within 30 days. Records we must keep by law (such as payment records held by Google) are kept by those providers.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
