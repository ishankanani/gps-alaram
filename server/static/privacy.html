Privacy

StopWake wakes you before your stop. It is built to need as little of your data as possible.

Your location stays on your phone

StopWake uses your location only on your device, to know when you are near your stop. Your location is never sent to our servers, and we do not keep a history of where you travel. Trip history in the app is stored on your phone only.

What we store on our server

  • Your StopWake ID (for example SW-7K3P-92QX) and a random install ID, so your plan works on this phone.
  • Your email address and name, only if you create an account.
  • Your plan: purchases made through Google Play (processed by Google and RevenueCat), gifted plans and redeemed codes.
  • App information: app version, Android version, language and country, used to show the right prices and to support you.

We do not collect contacts, photos or advertising IDs, and we do not sell your data.

Service providers

  • Google Play and RevenueCat process purchases and subscriptions.
  • Our email provider sends account emails such as password reset codes.
  • Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data). Like any website, the tile server sees your IP address.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept while you use StopWake. When you delete your account, it is removed at once; backups that still contain it expire within 30 days.

Your rights

You can see and export your data in the app (Account → Export my data), correct it (Account → Edit profile) and delete it (Account → Delete account). See also how to delete your account.
